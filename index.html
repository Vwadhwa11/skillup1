Questions teams ask us

Do we need technical experience?

No. AI at Work and AI for Business are designed for non-technical teams. AI Build Lab suits mixed groups: we pair confident builders with beginners and coach at both levels.

Can the training be customised to our company?

Yes. We run a short discovery call, then base exercises on your processes, terminology and policies. Prices are indicative, and we confirm a fixed quote after discovery.

Which tools will we use?

Where suitable we use Claude Code, Cursor, AntiGravity and Vercel, alongside the AI assistants and apps your team already has approved. We adapt to your security and licensing requirements.

Can you deliver remotely?

Yes. Sessions run live over video with breakout practice and shared build spaces. On-site delivery is available by arrangement and travel may be quoted separately.
